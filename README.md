PHISHING AWARENSS GUIDE 

PROJECT DESCRIPTION 

These project serves a purpose to educate users on how to identify , prevent and report phishing attackers. It focuses on raising cybersucurity awareness protecting personal information and encouraging safe online practices to reduce the risk of cybercrime.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

Educate society about phishing
How to identify suspicious emails , messages and links 
To promote safe internet and email practices.
To encourage the users and society to report
To reduce the risk of data theft , identity theft and online fraud.

Project contents 
Introduction to phishing 
Types of phishing attacks
Common signs of phishing.
Consequences of phishing attacks.
Phishing awareness and prevention.
Cybersecurity safety tips.
How to report phishing incidents.
Recommendations.
